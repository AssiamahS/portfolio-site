How this works

Zero human loop.

The engine is a Python and Playwright system I wrote and run myself. It finds postings, tailors a resume to each job description, grades the match, fills the form and presses submit. Nobody reviews a queue. If a step can't be done safely, the attempt is blocked and the reason lands on this page.

1 · Find and tailor

Postings come in from company career boards. For each one a resume is rebuilt against the job description and scored; anything under the bar never leaves.

2 · Fill and submit

A browser session fills the ATS form from an answer bank. No blind guesses on dropdowns or required questions; an unanswerable one blocks the attempt.

3 · Match receipts

The inbox is read for employer confirmations and rejections and each one is matched back to its application. "Confirmed" here means a real receipt, not a hopeful click.
